# Generation and provenance conventions

## Verification labels (never promote without evidence)

| Label | Meaning | Required evidence |
|---|---|---|
| GENERATED | Source/config exists | The files |
| COMPILED | Built by the real toolchain | Build command + output/log |
| SIMULATOR-TESTED | Launched on emulator/simulator | Launch log + screenshot artifact |
| PHYSICAL-DEVICE-TESTED | Ran on a real device | Device model, OS, screenshot/log |

A capability's label is the highest level with evidence. A failed or skipped step is reported as
such, with the exact command and error. Never write a result you did not observe.

## Generated vs hand-maintained

- Files produced by a generator or tool carry a header `GENERATED by <tool> <version> - do not edit`
  where the format allows, or live under a `generated/` directory.
- Hand-maintained files must not live under `generated/`.
- Gradle wrapper files are tool-generated and committed on purpose.

## Provenance of AI-authored content

- Specs under `.kiro/specs/` are the source of truth for generated game code.
- Generated assets record origin in `assets/PROVENANCE.md`: source, tool/model, license, date.
- Do not commit third-party assets without a recorded license.
- Device Lab reports are evidence: do not hand-edit them after the run.
